01 — Culinary & Hosting

Abenwaha

The table remembers what the record forgets.

Abenwaha carries the culinary and experiential arm of the house — food built on inherited technique, hosted as an occasion rather than served as a transaction. An all-day table, 9am to midnight, intimate enough to seat twenty and mean it, built around a single rule: everything tastes unmistakably Ghanaian, even the dishes that don't start out that way.

03 — Curation

WORBS Home

The table Abenwaha sets, built by hand first.

Every table needs somewhere to land the food, and every room needs something worth sitting in. WORBS Home builds the tables, chairs, and objects that make hosting look effortless — candles, incense, bakhoor, and pottery included — for anyone who wants the pieces without needing an occasion for them.

04 — Farm & Field

The Wranch

Where Abenwaha's table begins.

The Wranch is the house's working farm — produce, plants, and flowers grown, not just sourced, paired with a farmacy dispensing what the land yields and monthly hostings that bring people onto the soil itself. It carries the house's environmental conservation work and is the origin point for the clean eating Abenwaha puts on the plate.

05 — Philanthropy

True Religion

A door held open, not a hand held out.

True Religion exists to put resources, mentorship, and access in front of underprivileged girls before the world tells them what they can't do. A hands-on volunteer program gives girls practical experience before they choose their own path forward. It is the house's long-term ledger entry — the one measured in decades, not quarters.
